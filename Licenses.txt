Java 21 runtime: Eclipse Temurin OpenJDK 21.0.13+9 (Windows x64 JDK), downloaded from the official adoptium/temurin21-binaries release. GPLv2 with Classpath Exception; the downloaded archive retains its legal notices. Source and release: https://github.com/adoptium/temurin21-binaries/releases/tag/jdk-21.0.13%2B9 . The JDK includes jdk.jartool required by RSProx's JAR signer. This runtime is isolated to Voidburn and does not change system Java.
Voidburn MOTD plugin: com.voidburn.plugin, source under addons/motd; bundled thin JAR uses the running RuneLite's dependencies.
